## Volume responsiveness

- Volume changes update only audio gain and volume UI bindings. They no longer
  rebuild menus and shortcuts or reapply video decoder/cache preferences.
- Volume settings save after a brief pause in adjustment and preserve the final
  value when the app closes immediately after dragging.
- Audio gain changes use a short PCM ramp to reduce clicks. Muting and unmuting
  during playback keep the audio device and playback clock running.
- Regression checks cover preference isolation/persistence, continuous native
  audio output through volume changes, and dragging the slider during playback.

## Validation

- 28 core tests and 5 native audio tests passed.
- Full native UI smoke test passed, including mouse-driven volume adjustment
  during playback and an uninterrupted audio clock through mute/unmute.
- Maximum synchronous mouse-move handler time in that volume test was 0.265 ms
  on the development machine. This measures handler work, not end-to-end audio latency.
